17. DEVICE DESCRIPTION
The Vantage Orian (Model MRT-1550) is a 1.5 Tesla Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) System. The
Vantage Orian uses 1.4 m short and 3.8 tons light weight magnet. It includes the Pianissimo™
technology (scan noise reduction technology). The design of the gradient coil and the WB coil of the
Vantage Orian 1.5T provides the maximum field of view of 55 x 55 x 50 cm. The Model MRT-1550/AC,
AD, AG, AH includes the standard gradient system and Model MRT-1550/AK, AL, AO, AP includes the
XGO gradient system. The AiCE Reconstruction Processing Unit for MR is included with this system for
the processing of images for various anatomical regions.
This system is based upon the technology and materials of previously marketed Canon Medical Systems
MRI systems and is intended to acquire and display cross-sectional transaxial, coronal, sagittal, and
oblique images of anatomic structures of the head or body. The Vantage Orian MRI System is
comparable to the current 1.5T Vantage Orian MRI System (K193021), cleared June 3rd, 2020.

nuclei) that exhibit nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR). The NMR properties of body tissues and fluids
