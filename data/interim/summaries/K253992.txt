F. Device Description:
The Veritas Reverse Total Shoulder Arthroplasty System is intended for patients requiring a reverse
shoulder replacement for patients with a functional deltoid muscle and with a deficient rotator cuff.
The Veritas Reverse Total Shoulder Arthroplasty System consists of the patient-specific glenoid
baseplate, glenosphere intraoperatively affixed to the baseplate, humeral stem, and polymer bearing
component affixed to the humeral stem. Additionally, the system includes supporting
instrumentation, both patient-specific and standard instrument trays and all required accessories
(e.g., security screws, peripheral screws). The proposed device is a robust patient-specific offering for
a reverse total shoulder including baseplate offerings with patient specific backside geometry and
central fixation location.
The Veritas Reverse Total Shoulder Arthroplasty System includes an AI-based algorithm for auto
segmentation of the CT scans within the patient-specific software workflow. The auto segmentation
is intended to be used with human intervention for manual cleanup and review. Similar to the
indications for use of the Veritas Reverse Total Shoulder Arthroplasty, auto segmentation has only
been validated for use in primary shoulder arthroplasty. Ground truth was established through
manual segmentation of the CT Scans. The testing data was independent from the training and tuning
data, and testing data was from independent clinical sites. CT Scans were collected following the
proposed CT Scan protocol for the Veritas Reverse Total Shoulder System. The data set information is
provided in the table below.
The algorithm met predefined DICE score-based acceptance criteria of ≥ 0.87 compared to ground
truth manual segmentation and was consistent across Scapula (95% CI: 0.88), and Humerus (95% CI:
0.90). The algorithm also met predefined Mean Hausdorff Distance (MHD) based acceptance criteria
of ≤3.0 and the result was consistent across Scapula (95% CI: 0.98), and Humerus (95% CI: 0.68)
