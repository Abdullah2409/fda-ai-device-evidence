5. Device Description [21 CFR 807.92(a)(4)]
The uMR 680 is a 1.5T superconducting magnetic resonance diagnostic device with
a 70cm size patient bore. It consists of components such as magnet, RF power
amplifier, RF coils, gradient power amplifier, gradient coils, patient table,

Summary of the Machine Learning Algorithm (DeepRecon):
DeepRecon is a deep-learning based image processing algorithm for image de-noising
and K-space-interpolation based image super-resolution.
The training data of DeepRecon were collected from 264 volunteers. Each subject was
scanned by UIH MRI systems for multiple body parts and clinical protocols, resulted
in a total of 165,837 cases. In terms of the ground truth and input images in training
dataset, the multiple-averaged images with high-resolution and high SNR were
collected as the ground-truth images. The input images were generated from the
