Indications for Use (Describe)
AmCAD-UO is a PC-based, self-contained, non-invasive image analysis software application for reviewing the
pharyngeal/upper airway ultrasonic image acquired from an FDA-cleared ultrasound system. The software is designed to
support visualization and quantification of the airway sonographic characteristics. The software also provides tools for
manual or interactive selection of Region of Interest to allow for analysis of the airway in terms of its size, position and
enhancement pattern. This analysis provides information for physician’s evaluation and monitoring of the airway state.
