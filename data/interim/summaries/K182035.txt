5.4 Device Description
FFR is coronary physiologic simulation software developed for the clinical quantitative and
CT
qualitative analysis of CT DICOM data. It is a tool for the analysis of CT DICOM‐compliant cardiac
images and data, to assess the anatomy and function of the coronary arteries.
The software displays the anatomy combined with functional information using graphics and
text, including computed and derived quantities of blood flow, pressure and velocity, to aid the
clinician in the assessment of coronary artery disease.

5.7 Summary of Studies
The software was designed, developed, tested and validated according to written procedures.
